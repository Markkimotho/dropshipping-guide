The Definitive Dropshipping Blueprint: From Kenya to the World with a US LLC.

This interactive guide is your all-in-one resource to build a thriving dropshipping business, leveraging the power of a US Limited Liability Company (LLC) to streamline international payments and build unparalleled trust. We'll walk you through every step, from legal setup to scaling your sales.

What You'll Achieve with This Guide:

  • Establish a Solid Legal Foundation: Set up a US LLC for credibility and liability protection.
  • Master Global Payments: Access top-tier US payment gateways for seamless international transactions.
  • Build a High-Converting Store: Design a professional Shopify storefront that attracts and retains customers.
  • Source Winning Products: Discover fast-moving, high-demand items directly from China.
  • Streamline Operations: Automate order fulfillment and shipping for efficiency.
  • Navigate Taxes & Compliance: Understand and manage your tax obligations in both the US and Kenya.

Why I made this guide:

I spent a few years trying dropshipping. It did not become a successful business, but I learned resilience and how much the legal and payment setup matters. Learning to set up an LLC is one part of that experience I still value. These are notes from that attempt, not a promise of results.

Phase 1: Foundational Setup

This is the most critical phase. Setting up your US legal and banking structure correctly is paramount for international success. Start this immediately as it takes 4-8 weeks.

Form Your US LLC (Wyoming Recommended)

❯

A US LLC enhances credibility, gives access to better payment processors, and provides liability protection. Wyoming is the top choice for non-residents due to strong privacy laws, low fees, and no state income tax.

Action: Engage an LLC Formation Service

These services are essential for non-residents. They handle the complex legal filings, act as your required Registered Agent, and guide you through the process, including obtaining your EIN.

Recommended Services:

Highly recommended for non-US residents. Comprehensive packages for formation, EIN, and banking assistance.

Open a US Business Bank Account

❯

A US bank account is essential for using US payment processors like Shopify Payments and Stripe, separating finances, and appearing professional. It must be done after your LLC is formed and you have your EIN.

Action: Apply to an Online Bank Remotely

Traditional banks often require an in-person visit. Fintech banks are designed for remote, non-resident founders, making this step much easier.

Tax & Legal Compliance (US & Kenya)

❯

Navigating international tax laws is complex. Proper compliance is critical to avoid penalties and ensure long-term business viability.

Critical Action: Hire Tax Professionals

This is not optional. You **MUST** engage both a US tax accountant (for IRS filings) and a Kenyan tax consultant (for KRA filings and treaty benefits) to remain compliant and avoid severe penalties.
